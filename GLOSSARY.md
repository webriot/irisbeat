# Irisbeat

## Language

**Monitor**:
An HTTP, TCP or Heartbeat check with a fixed kind, its own identity and retained availability and Incident history.

**Monitor configuration**:
The Operator’s chosen name, target, publication and timing settings for a Monitor.

**Operator session**:
An operator’s authenticated visit to the Operator view, from sign-in until sign-out, authorization loss, or page reload.
